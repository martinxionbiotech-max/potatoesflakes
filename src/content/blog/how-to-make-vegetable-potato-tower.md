### Ingredients  
Main Ingredients
- 100 g [Buttermilk mashed potatoes](/product-category/instant-mashed-potato/)
- 50 g Carrot
- 50 g Green peas
Ingredients
- Egg mixture
- Plain pastry shell

### Equipment

- Normal Kitchenware

### Method 

1. Cut the carrots into small cubes and boil until cooked. Mix the cooked carrots and green peas into the buttermilk mashed potatoes 
1. Fill the pastry shell with the mixed mashed potatoes, brush with egg mixture, and bake in the oven at 180 degrees Celsius.
