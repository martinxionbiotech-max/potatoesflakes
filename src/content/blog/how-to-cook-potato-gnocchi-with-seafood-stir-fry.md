### Ingredients  
Main Ingredients
- 150 g Basic [mashed potatoes](/product-category/instant-mashed-potato/)
- 50 g Shrimp
- 50 g Scallops
- 50 g Mussels
- 50 g Cherry tomatoes 
Ingredients
- Flour
- Tomato sauce
- Olive oil
- Crushed black pepper
- Chopped onion

### Equipment

- Normal Kitchenware

### Method 

1. Mix basic mashed potatoes with flour, press into 1 cm thick slices, and cut into cubes.
1. Use a fork to create patterns on the cubes, boil them in water until they float, then remove and let them cool. Cook the shrimp, scallops, and mussels, set aside.
1. Stir-fry chopped onions in olive oil until fragrant, add the blanched seafood and cherry tomatoes, pour in the cooked potato gnocchi, add tomato sauce, and stir-fry evenly.
1. Season with salt and black pepper.
