## Pan-Seared Pork Chop with Mashed Potatoes and Vegetable Salad

### Ingredients  
Main Ingredients
- 150 g Pork chop
- 150 g [Buttermilk mashed potatoes](/product-category/instant-mashed-potato/)
- 50 g Shredded purple cabbage
- 50 g Shredded cabbage
Ingredients
- Salt
- Pepper powder
- Flour
- Egg
- Breadcrumbs
- Vegetable oil
- Salad dressing

### Equipment

- Normal Kitchenware

### Method 

1. Season the pork chop with salt, pepper, coat with flour, dip in beaten egg, then coat with breadcrumbs, and pan-sear until golden brown.
1. Mix the two kinds of shredded vegetables with salad dressing, serve with mashed potatoes.
