## Beef and Potato Gratin
"The carefully layered design always evokes thoughts of the accumulation of years, reminding us to cherish what we have now and be grateful for the beauty of life. While eating, it's about savoring food, but it's also about tasting life, while brewing up something wonderful."

### Ingredients  
Main Ingredients
- 150  g [Mashed potatoes](/product-category/instant-mashed-potato/) with butter and milk
- 150 g Beef sauce
Ingredients
- Parmesan cheese powder
- Breadcrumbs

### Equipment

- Normal Kitchenware

### Method 

1. In a bowl, layer the heated beef sauce followed by a layer of mashed potatoes with butter and milk, repeating this sequence for a total of three layers.
1. Sprinkle breadcrumbs and Parmesan cheese powder on top.
1. Bake in the oven at 180 degrees Celsius for 25 minutes or until the surface turns golden brown.
1. Carefully remove and place on a platter, garnish with chopped parsley.
