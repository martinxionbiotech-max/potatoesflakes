### Ingredients  
Main Ingredients
- 150 g Basic mashed potatoes
- 100 g Cheddar cheese
Ingredients
- Flour
- 2 Eggs
- Breadcrumbs
- Tomato sauce

### Equipment

- Normal Kitchenware

### Method 

1. Cut the Cheddar cheese into strips.
1. Wrap the Cheddar cheese strips with basic mashed potatoes and roll them into sticks. Coat each stick with flour, eggs, and breadcrumbs in sequence.
1. Fry in a deep fryer at 180 degrees Celsius until golden brown, melting the cheese.
1. Serve on a plate with tomato sauce.
