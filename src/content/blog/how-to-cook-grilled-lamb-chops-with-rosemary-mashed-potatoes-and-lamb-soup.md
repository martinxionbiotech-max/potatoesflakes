### Ingredients  

- 300 g Lamb chops
- 150 g [Buttermilk mashed potatoes](/product-category/instant-mashed-potato/) 
- 100 g Broccoli
- Salt
- Crushed black pepper
- Vegetable oil
- Lamb sauce
- Rosemary

### Equipment

- Normal Kitchenware
- Grill Oven

### Method 

1. The lamb chops are marinated with salt and crushed black pepper for flavor, and grilled to the desired doneness.
1. After boiling the broccoli, stir-fry it with vegetable oil, and season with salt and pepper.
1. Serve with mashed potatoes, pour lamb sauce over, and garnish with fresh rosemary sprigs.
