## What Are Instant Mashed Potatoes Made From?

[Instant mashed potatoes](/product-category/instant-mashed-potato/) are made from dehydrated potatoes. The process begins with fresh, high-quality potatoes that are peeled, cooked, mashed, and then dried into flakes or powder. These are rehydrated quickly with water, milk, or broth to give you fluffy mashed potatoes in minutes. Unlike some processed foods, instant mashed potatoes typically retain much of the natural goodness of real potatoes, including their flavor and nutrients.

## How Many Calories Are in Instant Mashed Potatoes?

The calorie count for instant mashed potatoes depends on how they’re prepared. When made with water alone, a 1-cup serving contains approximately 110 calories. Adding milk, butter, or cream will increase the calorie content. For example:

- Prepared with water: 110 kcal
- Prepared with 1 cup of milk: ~190 kcal
- Prepared with milk and butter: ~240 kcal

## How to Enjoy Instant Mashed Potatoes Healthily

To make your instant mashed potatoes healthier, consider these tips:

1. **Go Easy on the Butter and Cream:** Replace butter with olive oil for a heart-healthy fat option.
1. **Add Vegetables:** Mix in steamed broccoli, peas, or carrots to boost fiber and vitamins.
1. **Choose Low-Sodium Options:** If you’re watching your sodium intake, opt for low-sodium varieties or skip adding extra salt.
1. **Include Protein:** Top your mashed potatoes with grilled chicken, fish, or beans to make a balanced meal.

## Benefits and Drawbacks of Instant Mashed Potatoes

### Benefits:
- **Convenience:** Ready in minutes, perfect for busy days.
- **Versatility:** Can be customized with different ingredients to suit your taste.
- **Shelf-Stable:** A great pantry staple with a long shelf life.

### Drawbacks:
- **Sodium Content:** Many instant mashed potatoes contain added salt, which can be a concern for those with high blood pressure.
- **Potential Additives:** Some brands include preservatives or artificial flavors, so always read the label.
- **Texture:** While convenient, instant mashed potatoes may not have the same texture as freshly mashed potatoes.
