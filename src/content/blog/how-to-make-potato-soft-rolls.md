## Potato Soft Rolls
"This is a breakfast essential, isn't it? Carefully baked delicacies, paired with a glass of milk or soy milk, or perhaps a cup of delicious coffee or tea, can kickstart your day with energy and vigor.", Chinese

### Ingredients  
Main Ingredients
- 100 g Basic [mashed potatoes](/product-category/instant-mashed-potato/)
- 200 g Bread flour
- 300 g Milk
- 50 g Eggs
Ingredients
- Salt
- Yeast

### Equipment

- Oven

### Method 

1. Mix all ingredients except for the basic mashed potatoes to form a dough. Then add the basic mashed potatoes, let it rise, knead into small rolls, and let rise again.
1. Brush with beaten egg and bake in a preheated oven at 180 degrees Celsius for 25 minutes.
