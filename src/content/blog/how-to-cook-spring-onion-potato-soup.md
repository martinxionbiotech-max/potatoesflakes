### Ingredients  
Main Ingredients
- 150 g Basic [mashed potatoes](/product-category/instant-mashed-potato/)
- 300 g Water
- 100 g Light cream
Ingredients
- Spring onions
- Croutons

### Equipment

- Normal Kitchenware

### Method 

1. Bring water to a boil, add basic mashed potatoes and mix well, season when it boils, add cream, and immediately turn off the heat.
1. Strain through a fine sieve and ladle into soup bowls.
1. Drizzle with a spoonful of light cream, sprinkle with spring onions and crispy croutons.
