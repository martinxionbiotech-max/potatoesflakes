### Ingredients  
Main Ingredients
- 100 g Basic mashed potatoes
- 100 g Flour
- 200 g Milk
Ingredients
- Chocolate
- Baking soda

### Equipment

- Oven

### Method 

1. Mix basic mashed potatoes with flour and milk until well combined.
1. Transfer the mixture into a piping bag fitted with a nozzle, and pipe onto a baking tray.
1. Bake in a preheated oven at 180 degrees Celsius for 20 minutes.
