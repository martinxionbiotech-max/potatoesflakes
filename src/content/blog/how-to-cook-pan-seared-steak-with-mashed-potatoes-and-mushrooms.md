### Ingredients  
Main Ingredients
- 200g g Steak
- 150 g Butter milk mashed potato
- 150 g White mushrooms
Ingredients
- Salt
- White pepper
- Beef jus
- Vegetable oil
- Chopped herbs

### Equipment

- Normal Kitchenware

### Method 

1. Season the steak with salt and pepper, pan-sear until desired doneness.
1. Sauté the white mushrooms in oil until golden, season with herbs.
1. Serve with mashed potatoes and drizzle with beef jus.
