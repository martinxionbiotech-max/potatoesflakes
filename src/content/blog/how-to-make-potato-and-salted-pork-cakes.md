## Potato and Salted Pork Cakes
"You can taste the flavors your family used to cook with. Besides being surprised, we'll all eat more than we should. This dish should give you that feeling. In the familiar taste, we unknowingly end up eating a lot.", Chinese, Mexican

### Ingredients  
Main Ingredients
- 150 g Basic [mashed potatoes](/product-category/instant-mashed-potato/)
- 50 g Salted pork
Ingredients
- Chopped parsle
- [Potato flakes](/)

### Equipment

- Normal Kitchenware

### Method 

1. Chop the salted pork into small cubes and fry them in a pan until cooked, draining excess oil.
1. Mix the basic mashed potatoes with the cooked salted pork and chopped parsley, shape into cakes.
1. Coat with potato flakes and fry in a pan until golden brown.
