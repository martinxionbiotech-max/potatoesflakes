## Why are Most Chinese Potato Flakes Manufacturers Using European Equipment?

In China, the 42°N latitude region in the north is the main potato-producing area, providing a stable supply of raw materials for [potato flakes](/) processing. Many factories have been producing potato flakes for about 20 years, starting with equipment imported from Europe because Chinese manufacturers hadn’t yet developed suitable production lines. While some Chinese companies were working on it, the results weren’t satisfactory at the time. However, as China’s industrial capabilities have advanced, the country can now produce production lines of the same quality as those in Europe, but at more than 50% lower prices.

Therefore, it is expected that in the future, Chinese-made production lines will gradually replace imported ones, following the trend seen in many other industries.

## How Profitable is a Potato Flakes Production Project?

The production ratio of potato flakes is about 5:1, meaning 5 kg of potatoes yield 1 kg of potato flakes. The market price for 1 kg of potato flakes is significantly higher than that of 5 kg of potatoes. After deducting necessary costs, the profit from potato flakes is quite considerable.

For example, in 2024, the price of potatoes in China is about USD 0.28/kg. Therefore, 5 kg of potatoes cost only USD 1.4, while the price of 1 kg of potato flakes is USD 2.1.

Additionally, storing potatoes is much more challenging and costly than storing potato flakes. Generally, potato flakes are still in short supply in the Chinese market, and almost all manufacturers have stable production orders, indicating that the market has not yet reached its peak.

## Differences Between Chinese and European Production Lines

The main difference is in the details; Chinese-made production lines may still have some shortcomings in this regard. However, in terms of functionality, they are essentially the same.

The potato flakes production process involves steps like washing, peeling, cooking, and drying, each of which is akin to a small production line consisting of several machines working together. The quality of the production line largely depends on the quality and assembly of these individual machines.

If you have a sufficient budget, such as USD 2.5 million, manufacturers can use the best configurations to build your production line, making it competitive with European products.

## Specifications and Features of a China-Made Potato Flakes Production Line

The core part of potato flakes production is drum drying. The drying drum in our equipment has a diameter of 2 meters and a length of 5 meters, with a production efficiency of about 500 kg per hour. For higher efficiency, you can opt for a production line with 2-4 drying drums.

If your potato raw materials have a high moisture content, upgrading only the drying drum won’t suffice; you’ll also need to upgrade other parts of the production process. For instance, an Indian client who recently ordered a production line with two drying drums, capable of producing 1.2 tons of potato flakes per hour, also required an upgrade to the cooking equipment due to the relatively high moisture content of Indian potatoes.
