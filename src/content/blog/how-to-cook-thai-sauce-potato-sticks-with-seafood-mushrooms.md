## Thai Sauce Potato Sticks with Seafood Mushrooms
Very easy to cook and taste nice

### Ingredients  
Main Ingredients
- 150 g Basic [mashed potatoes](/product-category/instant-mashed-potato/)
- 150 g Seafood mushrooms
- 20 g Green garlic
- 20 g Red peper
- 20 g Green peper
Ingredients
- Thai chicken sauce
- Soy sauce
- Chicken powder
- Cornstarch

### Equipment

- Normal Kitchware

### Method 

1. Roll the basic mashed potatoes into sticks, coat with flour and fry until golden brown. Blanch the seafood mushrooms in boiling water and stir-fry, set aside.
1. n a pan, add base oil, Thai chicken sauce, minced garlic, green garlic, green and red peppers, stir-fry until fragrant. Add the seafood mushrooms and potato sticks, season, and stir-fry.
