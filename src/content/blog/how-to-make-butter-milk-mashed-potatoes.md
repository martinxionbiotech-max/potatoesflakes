## Make Butter Milk Mashed Potatoes
“Sweet and fluffy, always making one feel good, perhaps our mornings should start like this. Besides the nutrition we need, what we need more is this comforting feeling: lazy, natural.”Prep Time 5minsCook Time 5minsCourse: Breakfast, Dessert, Salad, Side DishCuisine: American, French, Italian

### Ingredients  

- 500 g Milk
- 100 g [Mashed potato powder](/product/original-instant-mashed-potato-powder/)
- 50 g Butter

### Equipment

- 1 White square deep salad bowl

### Method 

1. Heat milk in a pot until boiling
1. Gradually add mashed potato powder while stirring
1. Turn off the heat, add butter, and mix well
