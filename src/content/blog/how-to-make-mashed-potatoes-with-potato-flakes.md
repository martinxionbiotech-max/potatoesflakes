There are many ways to make [mashed potatoes](/product-category/instant-mashed-potato/), and using [potato flakes](/) is one of the most convenient methods.

## Using Potato Flakes to Make Mashed Potatoes

Generally, you only need 50 grams of potato flakes and 200 grams of hot water or hot milk. Pour the hot water or hot milk into the potato flakes, let it sit for 20 seconds, and then gently stir to get a fresh serving of plain mashed potatoes.

If you like, you can add ingredients like butter, cheese, chocolate, vegetables, strawberries, or blueberries to the plain mashed potatoes, then you will create unique flavored mashed potatoes.

You can enjoy a delicious serving of mashed potatoes as a breakfast or lunch supplement. For dinner, it paired with steak is one of your best choices.

Additionally, there are other methods to make it.

## Main Methods for Making Mashed Potatoes

1. **Potato Flakes** Our potato flakes are made from the Atlantic variety of potatoes. The product is pure white with almost no specks or black spots. The production includes a series of steps, including washing, peeling, steaming, mashing, and drying to produce the final product.
1. **Flavored Mashed Potato** Flavored mashed potatoes are made by evenly mixing the required seasoning powders with milled potato flakes using a centrifugal mixer. This method ensures that the seasoning is evenly distributed throughout the milled potato flakes.
1. **Potato Granules** Unlike potato flakes, potato granules are processed using a different production technique. The main difference lies in the dehydration method; potato granules are obtained through spray-drying. The granules are smaller and finer, providing a relatively better texture.
1. **Homemade** You can make mashed potatoes by peeling and boiling fresh potatoes, and then mashing them.

## Pros and Cons of These Methods

### Potato Flakes

**Advantages:**

- Highly rehydratable, allowing for quick and convenient preparation of plain mashed potatoes without any additives.
- More affordable than potato granules.
- Easy to store with a long shelf life.

**Disadvantages:**

- The texture made from potato flakes is not as smooth as those made from potato granules.

### Mashed Potato Powder

**Advantages:**

- Strong rehydration properties, allowing for instant dissolution and saving preparation time.
- Available in various flavors, enabling quick preparation of your preferred taste.
- Easy to store with a variety of flavor options.

**Disadvantages:**

- Fixed seasoning proportions mean it functions as a convenience food, which reduces the enjoyment of cooking and adjusting flavors.

### Fresh Potatoes (Homemade)

**Advantages:**

- More affordable and allows for flexible ingredient combinations.

**Disadvantages:**

- Time-consuming and complex to make.
- Short shelf life, prone to spoilage, and difficult to store.

### Potato Granules

**Advantages:**

- The best product for making mashed potatoes.
- Finer granules with a better texture.

**Disadvantages:**

- It is more expensive than others
