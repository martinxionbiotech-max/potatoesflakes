## 2024 Potato Raw Material Situation

Since October 2023, potato prices have been on the rise, particularly for processing potatoes. This trend has made it challenging for [potato flakes manufacturers](/product/) in China to secure sufficient raw materials, especially with significant price increases for Atlantic potatoes.

By September 2024, however, the prices of both potatoes and processing potatoes saw a considerable decline, leading to a reduction in manufacturers’ quotes, with average prices dropping by approximately $56-$63 per ton (€52-€59).

## Potato Flakes Prices in October 2024

Currently, the price for Atlantic [potato flakes](/) remains stable at $1,820 – $1,862 per ton (€1,690 – €1,726). In contrast, the prices for regular potato flakes are holding steady at $1,652 – $1,704 per ton (€1,550 – €1,564).

## Price Trends for Potato Flakes in 2025

Major manufacturers have adequate raw material reserves to ensure continuous production until March-April 2025. However, starting in May, the price of potato flakes is likely to fluctuate due to anticipated increases in potato procurement prices. Historical data suggests a strong likelihood of price increases, estimated to rise by 10%-15%.

This trend may continue until the end of September 2025, when new potato prices will significantly influence future potato flakes price movements. Due to a bountiful potato harvest in 2024, prices have remained low, but enthusiasm for potato planting in 2025 may wane, potentially leading to continued increases in raw material costs.
