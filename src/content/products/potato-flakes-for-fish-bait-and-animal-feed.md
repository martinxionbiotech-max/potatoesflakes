### 🌊 **Key Benefits in Fish Bait:**

- **Excellent Rehydration**: Quickly absorbs water and returns to a soft, palatable texture — perfect for attracting fish.
- **Fast Dispersion**: Enhances the clouding effect in water, creating a visual trail that lures fish effectively.
- **Natural Binder**: Improves bait cohesion while remaining easy to break down in water.
- **Digestible and Safe**: Easily consumed by fish, with no harmful additives.

### 🐄 **Advantages in Animal Feed:**

- **Highly Digestible Carbohydrates**: Provides a clean, energy-rich base for livestock and aquatic animals.
- **Improves Feed Texture**: Adds bulk and improves the binding of pellets without affecting palatability.
- **Non-GMO and Clean Label**: A safe, plant-based ingredient suitable for a wide range of animal species.
- **Shelf-Stable & Easy to Store**: Long-lasting flakes with excellent stability under standard feed storage conditions.
