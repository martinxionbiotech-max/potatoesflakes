## Product Description
- Milky white color
- It is made by grinding potato flakes, with no other additives.
- We primarily use milled potato flakes in the production of seasoned [mashed potatoes](/product-category/instant-mashed-potato/).
- It is very similar to potato flour so you can use it as a substitute.
- Milled potato flakes have a higher density, and the common package is 25 kg (55.1lb) bags for commercial purposes.
- The particle size of our milled potato flakes ranges from 80 mesh to 100 mesh.
- A 20-foot container can hold 13 tons.
- A 40-foot container can hold 22 tons.
- Large bulk bags can be chosen for packaging.
