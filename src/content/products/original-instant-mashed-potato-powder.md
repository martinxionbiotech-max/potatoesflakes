### Instructions
1. Bring 80 degrees Celsius water or milk to a boil.
1. Add water or milk and potato powder in a ratio of 1:5 (e.g., 1 cup water or milk to 5 cups potato powder).
1. Let stand for one minute.
1. Stir evenly with a whisk or fork until smooth and creamy.
